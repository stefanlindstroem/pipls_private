# Decision 0089: balanced Pulp factorization views

## Status

Accepted and implemented.

## Context

The complete Pulp example plots all four Pi-PLS factor views: predictor directions $P$, dilation
$D$, response directions $Q$, and weighted response directions $QD$. Tutorial 2 retained only the
$P$ plot. That choice showed the predictor side of the fitted factorization but omitted the
response-side quantity that combines direction and mode strength.

The focused tutorial should remain shorter than the complete example, but its Pi-PLS-specific
section should not reduce the factorization to one predictor-side view.

## Decision

Retain the predictor-direction $P$ plot and add a standalone weighted-response-direction $QD$ plot
to Tutorial 2. Generate the new chart directly from
`PiPLSDisplayFactors.weighted_response_directions` in `tools/render_pulp_tutorial.py`, and source
the displayed recipe from a checked renderer block.

Keep the separate $D$ and $Q$ plots in the complete numbered example and the model-inspection
reference rather than adding the full four-chart catalogue to the tutorial. State this explicitly
beside the two displayed factorization views.

The Pulp tutorial renderer therefore produces eight single-chart SVG outputs.

## Consequences

- Tutorial 2 shows both predictor-side orientation through $P$ and response-side orientation and
  strength through $QD$.
- Every displayed factorization figure still has a matching standalone Matplotlib recipe.
- The tutorial remains representative rather than duplicating the complete example's four-panel
  Pi-PLS factorization figure.
- The public inspection API, numbered-example PDF outputs, and rendering ownership are unchanged.
- Generated tutorial assets remain ignored working-tree products and are regenerated by the
  documentation targets.
